# Derived comparison tables — ExLlamaV3 1.5.3 on RTX 3060

Source: raw records of the official sessions, filtered to non-warmup runs.
Every figure is a **median of 10 measured runs**.

Provenance: aggregate throughput and decode throughput are the harness's own
`aggregate_output_tok_s` (from actual concurrent group wall-clock, never a sum of
isolated runs) and `decode_tok_s`. The columns marked **DERIVED** are computed
here because the harness leaves the corresponding fields null:
`C1→C8 factor = agg(C8) / agg(C1)`, `efficiency vs ideal 8× = factor / 8`,
`C1→C8 retention = per-request decode(C8) / per-request decode(C1)`.
